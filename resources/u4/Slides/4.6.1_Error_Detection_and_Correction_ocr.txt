Parity checking

We send 1011 0110 with an even-parity bit. Two bits flip on the way. What does the receiver
conclude?

a Error detected, and the frame is dropped b Error detected, and the bad bit is fixed

¢ No error detected: the parity still matches d Error detected, but only if the flips are adjacent

Step 1 Data has 5 ones - parity bit = 1 = 6 ones in total (even).

Step 2 Two flips change the count by -2, 0 or +2 - still even = check passes.

Answer: (c). One parity bit catches any odd number of flips and misses any even number.
