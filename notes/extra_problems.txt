Q1] Suppose that the packet length is 4000 bits, and
that the link transmission rate along the link from the
first router to the second is 10 MBps. What is the
transmission delay?
• L = 4000 bits
• Transmission Rate, R = 10 MBps = 10 x 8 x 106 bps = 80Mbps
• Transmission Delay = L/R = 4000 / (10 x 8 x 106)
= 0.00005s = 50 microseconds
