Signalling Methods

NRZ (Non Return to Zero) Encoding – A
low voltage being 0 and a higher voltage
representing a 1.

Manchester Encoding
• A voltage change from low to high within the
bit time represents a 1.
• A voltage drop within the bit time from a
high to a low voltage represents a 0.
