Longest prefix matching
• we’ll see why longest prefix matching is used shortly, when we study
addressing
• longest prefix matching: often performed using ternary content
addressable memories (TCAMs)
• content addressable: present address to TCAM: retrieve address in one clock cycle,
regardless of table size
• Cisco Catalyst: ~1M routing table entries in TCAM
